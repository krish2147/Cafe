Questions

Asked often enough to answer here.

Are you really open until 2am?

Every day. Eight in the morning to a quarter to midnight, then midnight to two. The kitchen slows down late but the counter does not.

What should I order?

Start with the Dunes Specialties. The Arabic Qahwa is brewed with cardamom and saffron and comes with dates, and the Date Latte is the one people order twice. The plates are Continental and built for a long sit rather than a quick one.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. There is no meat anywhere on the menu.

How much is it for two?

About ₹1,200 for two on a normal visit. Pre-booked tables carry a discount through District.

Can I come on my own and work?

Yes, and plenty of people do, mostly after nine. Indoor seating, and the later it gets the quieter it is.

Do I need to book?

Not on a weekday. Friday and Saturday after eight, yes. Booking also gets you the table you actually want rather than the one that happens to be free.

Where exactly are you?

FF-105 and 106, Neptune Trion, Alembic Gorwa Road, Alkapuri, Vadodara, Gujarat.
